# Source and credit

The asd-ste100 skill was written by Dustin Yuchen Teng. All credit for the rules
in `SKILL.md`, `references/writing-rules.md`, and `examples/before-after.md`
belongs to them. The copy in this repository is unmodified.

The skill itself applies ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, a controlled
language standard owned by ASD, the AeroSpace and Defence Industries Association
of Europe. The skill does not reproduce the standard. It restates the rule
categories and applies the idea behind them. The standard is free to download at
https://www.asd-ste100.org/ and you need it when exact approved wording matters.
